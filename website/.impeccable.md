## Design Context

### Users
Varsity Mart serves tertiary students and vendors. On auth screens, users are preparing to buy goods, list items for sale, or manage their vendor-side selling workflows.

### Brand Personality
The brand should feel **Youthful**, **African**, and **Tertiary**. The experience should feel energetic, culturally grounded, and campus-centered while still feeling trustworthy and clear.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a modern, lively direction aligned with student commerce in African tertiary communities. Keep interfaces visually warm and approachable, while avoiding heavy decorative framing.

Anti-reference:
- Avoid excessive borders everywhere.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize quick entry into core actions (buying and selling) with low-friction forms.
2. Keep visuals expressive and youthful without sacrificing legibility or clarity.
3. Reflect an African campus-market identity through tone and styling choices.
4. Maintain clean hierarchy and avoid noisy chrome, especially overuse of borders.
5. Ensure components stay accessible and consistent across auth flows.
